H-COM

Gameplay Information
Welcome to H-COM!

Hardcore Combat Overhaul Mod aims to bring as much realism to Planetary Annihilation as possible — AT ALL COSTS!

Features:

- Definitely not balanced gameplay... (I tried, though!)
- Cool features and assets borrowed from some of the best PA mods out there
- Completely reworked energy economy
- Separate air and surface vision layers, with orbital units no longer permanently visible
- Extensive use of armor classes, target priorities, and target exemptions
- Powerful new weapon systems and defensive countermeasures
- Devastating experimental technology
- Researchable unit upgrades and upgradable turrets
- Adjusted unit transport sizes and transporter capacities
- Complete gameplay overhaul — PLEASE READ UNIT DESCRIPTIONS!

Forget what you know about vanilla PA, and feel free to experiment!
